Calculation notes & sources

What is and isn't included

Honing math: 1700→1710 and 1710→1720 each use 10 Advanced Honing levels per piece, modeled with the updated Ancestor’s Grace rate. From 1720 onward, each +10 bracket requires two regular honing successes per piece. Regular honing success consumes a stochastic number of attempts. After every failed attempt, the success chance gains 10% of the original chance up to double the base; failed attempts grant Artisan’s Energy equal to the attempt’s chance × 0.465. The tap after reaching 100% Artisan’s Energy is guaranteed. Expected taps are calculated by summing the survival probability of reaching every tap. Regular honing Gear XP (shards and silver) is charged once per successful honing level; it is not charged per attempt. Lv. 3 / Lv. 4 Advanced Honing scrolls, Hellfire [19–20] books, and Tremor [12–15] / [16–19] books are applied on all eligible attempts; breaths, Special Honing currency, and event bonuses remain excluded. Advanced Honing is included at 1700–1720; this is not the same as using Special Honing tokens.

Important 1780 limitation: upper gear +21 requires Breakthrough after +20. The extra one-time materials/fees for each of the six gear pieces could not be independently confirmed; 1770→1780 and all cumulative totals reaching 1780–1800 are underestimates until you add that cost. One-time Serca Agony Thorn farming is also unpriced.

Market snapshot: EU Central prices from the October 22, 2026 reward-sheet benchmark, not up-to-the-second LOA Buddy data. Confirm or sync prices before spending. Gold values and bonus chest costs come from the October 2026 Western rewards sheet; market values move continually.

Gear path assumption: the 1700 start assumes regular Hellfire honing +18 and Advanced Honing +20. The normal alt route shown is Advanced Honing +21–30 (1700→1710), Advanced Honing +31–40 (1710→1720), regular honing +19/+20 (1720→1730), then crafted Destined Tremor gear (+11) honing to +25 (1800). All six gear pieces are honed evenly. This route assumes the alt already has Advanced Honing +20 at 1700. Advanced Honing uses full scrolls with the paid tap average from the supplied Honing Forecast screenshot for +21–30. The same full-scroll profile is provisionally applied to +31–40; this is not a second independently verified forecast. Each +10 range is a standalone step in this exact route.

Progression not priced automatically: remaining Ancient gear crafting items/Agony Thorns, pre-1700 Advanced Honing 1–20 if incomplete, +20 Breakthrough materials/costs, optional breaths, book purchase prices not established on the EU benchmark, other honing bonuses, bus fees, gems, accessories, elixirs, Ark Grid, Vambrace, auction income, daily tradeable materials, and the market value of silver are excluded. Silver is displayed as a resource, not converted to gold. Partial upgrade distributions and existing Artisan are not modeled: start at a full 10-item-level breakpoint with six equal pieces.

Incremental ROI definition: with material valuation off, payback weeks = honing investment ÷ (weekly raid gold at target − weekly raid gold at start). With materials on, add the difference in weekly ordinary raid-drop value plus purchased bonus-chest value minus chest gold costs at the target and starting levels. This measures the incremental return from upgrading an existing alt. “Gross income payback” divides by total target raid earnings (plus target materials if enabled) and is shown only for comparison; it overstates the financial return to honing. A no-change weekly value means no measurable incremental ROI. Optional material income is economic value usable for that alt, not money you can necessarily transfer to the roster. Values are estimates rather than guarantees.

Weekly raid materials and purchased chests: source-backed clear and bonus-chest quantities for each of the best three eligible gold raids replace the previous illustrative item-level material profiles. At 1710 the lineup includes newly unlocked Serca and Final Day, at 1720 upgraded Act 4 and Cathedral are available, and later raid difficulties unlock at 1730, 1740, 1750, 1770 and 1780; 1790/1800 unlock no additional raid in this model. The model values the raid's ordinary honing materials and, if enabled, the materials inside each purchased gate chest, then deducts every purchased chest's gold cost. “Buy profitable chests” only buys gate chests whose priced honing mats exceed their cost. This is net replacement value, not liquid gold; a bound material is useful only if the alt can spend it. Older T4 stones and leapstones are converted at 5:1 for valuation on an alt at 1730+; shards retain their own price. Fused leapstones, Ark Grid, Vambrace, first clears, daily content, and event rewards are excluded. Gold differences and material differences are each evaluated against the alt at its starting item level.

Price policy: default material and Lv3/Lv4 scroll prices match the 22 October 2026 EU benchmark in the linked ImDefild Bonus Chest Guide; Hellfire [19–20] book prices were not listed in that benchmark, so the default book opportunity cost for Hellfire and Tremor books is 0 until set manually (0 means bound or unknown, not free market supply), not current live quotes; live syncing requires a same-origin or CORS-enabled proxy. An optional Cloudflare Worker is included for that purpose. Before publishing a recommendation, update every material from your own EU region market, including Destiny Shards (which are not directly tradeable; the entered price is a pouch-equivalent valuation). Buying all materials means paying their replacement cost, not that every input can always be bought as an individual market item. Gold-only assumes all mats are already owned/bound; it is still an economic resource expenditure.
